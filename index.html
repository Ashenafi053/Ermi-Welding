About Ermi Welding

At Ermi Welding we turn steel into things people trust every day: gates that protect your home, handrails that keep your family safe, doors and windows built to last, and tables and beds made to your taste. Our workshop is at Adama Asala Bus Station.

Every piece is measured, cut and welded with care, because we want you to be proud of it for years. Good work should not cost a fortune, so we keep our prices fair.
