About Ermi Welding

At Ermi Welding, we turn steel into things people trust every day: strong gates that protect your home, handrails that keep your family safe, doors and windows built to last, and custom tables and beds made to your taste. Our workshop is at Adama Asala Bus Station (አዳማ፣ አሰላ መናኸሪያ).

Welding is more than a job for us; it is a craft we take pride in. Every piece is measured, cut and welded with care, because we want you to be proud of it for years. Good work should not cost a fortune, so we keep our prices fair. Your order can be made at our workshop, or we can come to your place, and we welcome partners to work with us on projects.
